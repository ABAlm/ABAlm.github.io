NASA JPL Mars Hard Lander – Electrical System

NASA JPL-Sponsored Senior Capstone Project

This capstone project, sponsored by NASA’s Jet Propulsion Laboratory (JPL), focuses on designing a Mars hard lander electrical system capable of surviving high-G impact conditions while autonomously collecting and transmitting environmental data from the Martian surface.

My primary responsibilities focused on the electrical subsystem, including power system architecture, Li-ion battery pack and BMS integration, power budgeting, and antenna/RF PCB design using Altium Designer.

System Architecture

The Mars hard-lander node combines sensing, processing, power management, and RF communication into a compact, impact-survivable electrical system.

The high-level architecture includes:

  • STM32-based processing and control
  • Environmental and seismic sensors
  • Custom 3S3P Li-ion power subsystem with BMS
  • Multi-rail power conversion (12 V, 5 V, 3.3 V)
  • Antenna and RF front-end PCB for communication

The electrical system was designed to support multi-sol operation while maintaining reliability under constrained power and thermal conditions.

My Role & Responsibilities

Power Subsystem Lead

Designed the 3S3P Li-ion battery configuration and integrated a BMS for protection, balancing, and safe charge/discharge behavior.

Developed and refined the system power budget to support multi-sol operation.

PCB Designer

Created the PCB layout for the antenna/RF front-end and contributed to power distribution, grounding, and decoupling strategies using Altium Designer.

Ensured proper routing and integration with the STM32-based main system board.

Power Conversion & Budgeting

Selected and analyzed buck converters to derive regulated 5 V and 3.3 V rails from the main battery bus.

Modeled current draw and subsystem duty cycles to verify compliance with energy and thermal constraints.

3S3P Li-ion Battery & BMS

The power subsystem is built around a custom 3S3P Li-ion battery pack designed to support extended operation under expected mission loads.

  • Assembled and welded the 3S3P battery pack
  • Integrated BMS protection and balancing
  • Designed safe charging and discharge paths
  • Validated performance under expected load conditions

Antenna & RF PCB Design

I designed the antenna/RF PCB responsible for interfacing the transceiver with the external antenna while supporting signal integrity and impedance matching.

  • Schematic capture and PCB layout in Altium Designer
  • RF component placement and routing
  • Ground planes and decoupling strategies
  • STM32 integration and connector interfacing

Testing & Validation

I contributed to subsystem testing and validation to reduce electrical and power-related risks.

  • Battery discharge testing using INA219 logging
  • BMS cutoff threshold verification
  • Power budgeting and current analysis
  • Subsystem integration testing

  • Power subsystem implementation

  • Testing procedures and final results
