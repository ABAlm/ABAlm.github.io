NASA JPL Mars Hard Lander – Avionics System

NASA JPL-Sponsored Senior Capstone Project

This capstone project, sponsored by NASA’s Jet Propulsion Laboratory (JPL), focused on developing a Mars hard lander avionics system capable of surviving high-G impact conditions while autonomously collecting, storing, and transmitting environmental data from the Martian surface.

My primary responsibilities focused on PCB development, power system testing, STM32H7 and STM32L4 firmware, and RTOS-based scheduling for communication and task coordination between the low-power controller and main processor.

My Role & Responsibilities

PCB Designer

Worked on the final avionics PCB integration, routing considerations, board validation, and hardware-level system integration.

Power System Testing

Validated the 1S9P Li-ion battery subsystem, power distribution, voltage regulation, and runtime stability under expected mission loads.

STM32 Firmware & RTOS

Developed STM32H7 and STM32L4 firmware with RTOS scheduling and L4-to-H7 task coordination for sensing, storage, and communication operations.

Final PCB & Embedded Integration

The final avionics PCB integrates embedded processing, power management, sensing systems, and communication interfaces into a compact impact-survivable embedded system.

  • STM32H7 + STM32L4 coordination
  • RTOS task scheduling architecture
  • Sensor integration and communication buses
  • Power system monitoring and validation
